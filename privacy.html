Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 September 2026

This policy explains what data is handled by Tukim Tovim (“we”, “us”), a Hebrew-language political satire channel, and by our internal application Tukim Tovim Publisher.

1. Who we are

Tukim Tovim is an independent satire channel producing short-form video. We are the sole operator of the channel and of the application described below. Contact: tukimtovim@gmail.com.

2. What Tukim Tovim Publisher does

Tukim Tovim Publisher is a first-party publishing tool. It is used only by us, only to upload our own original video content to social accounts that we own and operate. It is not offered to the public, it does not accept sign-ups, and it does not act on behalf of any third party.

3. Data the application accesses

  • Basic account information of our own connected accounts — account identifier, display name and avatar — used to confirm the tool is connected to the correct account before uploading.
  • Upload capability — the application submits video files and their captions to our own accounts. On TikTok it submits content to the account inbox as a draft; a human then reviews and publishes it manually.
  • Publishing status of the content we submitted, so we can tell whether an upload succeeded.

4. Data we do not collect

  • We do not collect, request, store or process data belonging to other users of any platform.
  • We do not collect personal data from visitors to this website. This site is static, has no accounts, no forms, no analytics and no advertising.
  • We do not use cookies or any similar tracking technology.
  • We do not use any platform data to train or fine-tune machine learning models.

5. Credentials and storage

Access tokens for our own accounts are stored locally on the operator’s machine, in a file readable only by the operating-system account of the operator, and are excluded from version control. Tokens are never transmitted to any third party, never shared, and never published. They are used solely to authenticate requests to the platform that issued them.

6. Retention and deletion

We retain access tokens only while the integration is in active use. Tokens are deleted when they are revoked or no longer needed. Publishing status records are operational logs kept locally and are deleted when no longer required.

7. Revoking access

Because the application only ever accesses accounts we own, access can be revoked by us at any time from the settings of the relevant platform. Doing so immediately and permanently ends the application’s access.

8. Third parties

We do not sell, rent, trade or otherwise disclose any data to third parties. The application communicates only with the official APIs of the platforms on which we publish.

9. Children

Our content and this application are not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect any data relating to children.

10. Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new “last updated” date.

11. Contact

Questions about this policy: tukimtovim@gmail.com
